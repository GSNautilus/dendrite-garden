Field guide
Glowing branches are dendritic voltage. Input light drains toward the soma and fades with distance.
Hot flares are NMDA dendritic spikes: a branch firing on its own when coincident input arrives.
A flash spreading outward from a soma is a back-propagating action potential. It is also the teaching signal for plasticity.
Comets are spikes in flight down axons. Warm ones excite, cool ones inhibit.
Beads are synapses. Size is strength, flicker is short-term facilitation or fatigue, and a four-point star is a consolidated long-term memory.
Soma hue follows each neuron's firing phase, so neurons in sync share a colour.
